How IGV works for municipalities

Designed for partnerships.

Solving the housing crisis requires more than builders, more than capital, and more than policy. It requires all parties working together toward a common outcome.

What makes a successful municipal partnership.

The strongest housing outcomes occur when municipalities, local communities, private capital, and housing providers work toward a shared goal. IGVhousing seeks to partner with municipalities that are committed to increasing housing supply, expanding homeownership, and delivering measurable community outcomes.

Prioritize housing supply and affordability

Support streamlined and predictable approval pathways

Support energy-efficient, high-quality housing outcomes

Leverage private capital alongside public initiatives to maximize housing delivery

Embrace innovative construction methods and modern housing solutions

Seek pathways to increase homeownership for local residents and essential workers

Focus on measurable outcomes rather than ongoing subsidy dependence

Share a commitment to building stronger, more resilient communities
